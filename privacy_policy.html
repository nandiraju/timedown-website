1. Introduction & Core Principles

TimeDown is developed by Srikanth Nandiraju. We believe that personal event countdowns—such as birthdays, weddings, anniversaries, vacations, and personal goals—are private. The application is built with a local-first architecture so that you never have to trust an external cloud server with your personal calendar milestones.

2. Information Handled by the App

When using TimeDown on iOS or Android:

  • Event Names & Notes: Stored strictly inside the app's private on-device storage.
  • Target Dates & Times: Stored locally to calculate real-time countdown intervals.
  • App Preferences: Display settings stored locally on your device.

We do not operate backend servers that receive, copy, or process your event data. If you delete an event or uninstall the application, the local data is deleted.

3. What We Never Collect

  • No user registration, passwords, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • No GPS or location tracking.
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, advertising ID on Android) or cross-app tracking.
  • No monetization of personal data or sale to data brokers.

4. Website Analytics & Cookies

The TimeDown app collects no data. This website (timedown.cend.in), operated by Srikanth Nandiraju, uses Google Analytics 4, provided by Google, with cookies to understand how visitors use the site: pages viewed, approximate location (country or city derived from your IP address by Google), device and browser type, referring site, and clicks on the App Store and Google Play buttons. We use this only to improve the website. Advertising features are switched off: no ad cookies, no ad personalization, and no data shared for advertising.

Your choice: visitors in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see a banner, and analytics cookies are set only after you click Accept. Elsewhere, analytics is on by default. You can change your choice at any time with the button below, block cookies in your browser, or install Google's Analytics opt-out add-on. Google processes this data under its Privacy Policy.
