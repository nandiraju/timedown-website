FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CounttMinus completely free? +

Yes! CounttMinus is free to download on the Apple App Store without subscriptions or annoying paywalls.

Do I need to register or create an account? +

No registration required. Open the app, tap the `+` button, and start creating countdowns immediately.

How many event countdowns can I create? +

As many as you want! There is no limit on the number of countdowns you can track simultaneously.

Where is my event data saved? +

All your countdown events and notes are saved 100% locally on your iPhone or iPad. Your dates are private to you.

Which devices are supported? +

CounttMinus is universal for iOS and iPadOS devices running iOS 15.1 or later.
