FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is TimeDown completely free? +

Yes! TimeDown is free to download on the Apple App Store and Google Play without subscriptions or annoying paywalls.

Do I need to register or create an account? +

No registration required. Open the app, tap the `+` button, and start creating countdowns immediately.

How many event countdowns can I create? +

As many as you want! There is no limit on the number of countdowns you can track simultaneously.

Is TimeDown available on Android? +

Yes. TimeDown is on Google Play for Android and on the Apple App Store for iPhone and iPad, so your countdowns look and feel the same on both.

Where is my event data saved? +

All your countdown events and notes are saved 100% locally on your iPhone, iPad or Android device. Your dates are private to you.

Which devices are supported? +

TimeDown is available for iPhone and iPad (iOS 15.1 or later) on the Apple App Store, and for Android devices on Google Play.
